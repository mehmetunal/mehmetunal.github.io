FAQ (EN)

  • How do I play vs bots? From the hub open Bot or PLAY NOW — pick difficulty, color, and clock.
  • Create a table? Create Table → pick a clock → share the code. You can practice vs a bot if the seat is empty.
  • How does Elo work? Elo updates after bot matches; the leaderboard shows your score.
  • How do I get chips? Match rewards, daily login, and rewarded ads; packs via App Store / Play when available.
  • No ads? Buy the one-time No-Ads product in the shop where available.
  • Delete account? Settings → Delete account.
  • Ads? No banners; rewarded and post-match interstitial (1 / 3 / 5…).
