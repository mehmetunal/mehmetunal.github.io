Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Santranç Online (“the app”, bundle / package: com.santranc.satrancOnline / com.santranc.satranc_online) is a free mobile chess game by Mehmet Ünal. Guests can play bots and puzzles; cloud accounts use Firebase Authentication (email / Google / Apple).

Data we collect

  • Account (Firebase Auth): Guest, email, Google, or Apple sign-in. A user ID is required for profile sync.
  • Profile (Firestore): Display name, avatar, Elo, chip balance, cosmetics / No-Ads status, and related game fields.
  • Game data: Match/session records, puzzle progress, and local preferences (SharedPreferences) may be stored on device.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics — we do not intentionally send PII such as email or real name; events are in-game metrics.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Rewarded and interstitial ads may show at appropriate moments. No banners / app-open ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and measurement data. UMP consent may be used in the EEA. Test units may be used in development.
  • Purchases: Chip packs and a one-time No-Ads product may be offered via App Store / Google Play. Card details are not stored in the app; stores verify transactions.

Data we do not collect

  • No location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.
  • No free-text chat; social interaction is smile / gift oriented.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Auth, Firestore, Analytics
  • Google AdMob — ads
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution and in-app purchases
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — authentication

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Deleting data

Uninstalling removes local data. Account deletion: Settings → Delete account. For cloud data requests, contact us by email.
