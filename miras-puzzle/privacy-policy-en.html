Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Miras Puzzle (“the app”) is a free jigsaw puzzle game developed by Mehmet Ünal. It needs no account; all photos ship inside the app and no internet connection is needed to play.

Data we collect

  • Local game data: Completed puzzles, stars, moves and time are stored only on your device and never sent to a server.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): A full-screen interstitial ad may be shown when you finish a puzzle. AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address and ad interaction data for ad delivery, fraud prevention and measurement.
  • Tracking permission (iOS): The app asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission for personalised ads. If you decline, non-personalised ads are shown and the game works the same.
  • GDPR / UMP: Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for ad consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice from the “Gizlilik ayarları” (Privacy settings) link on the home screen.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, email, name or phone number.
  • No camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or precise location access.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery (how Google uses data)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution

Children

The game suits all ages but is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and ad content is limited to the general-audience (G) rating.

Images

Puzzle photos come from openly licensed sources. Miras Puzzle is not an official UNESCO app and is not affiliated with UNESCO.

Deleting data

Uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings to remove local game data. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page.
