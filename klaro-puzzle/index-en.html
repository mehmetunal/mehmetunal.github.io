Klaro Puzzle

Adventure Map, void boards, crystals and walls

An original drag-and-drop grid puzzle built from scratch in Flutter — not a template. The app opens on the Adventure Map. Void cells reshape the board (L-shapes, donut layouts); crystals break when their row or column clears; stone walls stay in place.

  • Adventure Map — 25 handcrafted levels in five themed regions
  • Void cells — permanent holes, not a plain 8×8
  • Crystal goals and stone walls
  • Classic Endless in a separate menu — in-place line clears
  • Turkish / English · AdMob (UMP / ATT)
