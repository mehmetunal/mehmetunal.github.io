Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Tavla Online (“the app”, bundle: com.tavlaonline.tavlaOnline) is an online/offline backgammon game by Mehmet Unal.

Data we collect

  • Account / profile: Display name, avatar, chip balance, friendships and match history stored in Firebase Authentication / Firestore.
  • Gameplay & social: Match state, stickers/chat, invites — for app functionality.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics events (aggregated).
  • Purchases: Apple / Google store transactions for chips and No-Ads. Payment card data is not sent to the app.
  • Tracking (iOS ATT) / UMP: Personalized ads may request ATT; UMP consent is shown in GDPR regions.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob / Firebase
  • Apple (App Store / IAP)
