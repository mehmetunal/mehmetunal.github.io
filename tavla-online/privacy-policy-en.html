Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Kral Tavla (“the app”, bundle / package: com.tavlaonline.tavlaOnline / com.tavlaonline.tavla_online) is a free mobile backgammon game by Mehmet Ünal. Guest play supports bot matches; live online features require Firebase Auth with a username.

Data we collect

  • Account (Firebase Auth): Username and password for online tables, friends, and cloud profile. Guest sessions stay on device only.
  • Profile (Firestore): Display name, avatar, chip balance, purchase / No-Ads status, friend relationships, and score / Elo fields used for leaderboards.
  • Game data (Firestore): Open table list, player status, public table / game state, and short-lived smile reactions.
  • Push (FCM): If notification permission is granted, a device token may be stored (invites / social alerts).
  • Local settings: Preferences and guest profile may be stored on device (SharedPreferences).
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Rewarded and interstitial ads may show at appropriate moments. No banners. AdMob may collect device identifiers and measurement data. Test ad units may be used in development.
  • Purchases: A one-time No-Ads product may be purchased via App Store / Google Play. Chips are not sold for real money; transactions are verified through the stores. Card details are not stored in the app.
  • Analytics / crashes: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect anonymous / aggregated events for diagnostics and improvement.

Data we do not collect

  • No location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.
  • No free-text chat; social interaction is smile / gift oriented.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Auth, Firestore, Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Functions
  • Google AdMob — ads
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution and in-app purchases

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Deleting data

Uninstalling removes local data. For account / cloud deletion, use the in-app option in settings or contact us by email.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is published on this page.
