Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Batak: Koz Kralı (“the app”, iOS and Android package com.ggamgames.kozkrali) is a free card game developed by Mehmet Ünal. You can play offline against the computer as a guest, without an account; online tables and the leaderboard require one. This page explains which data is used and why.

Data we collect

  • Account (Firebase Authentication): You can sign up with a username and password. Technically the username maps to an address such as username@kozkrali.app; no real email is requested and no mail is sent to it. With Sign in with Apple or Google, the account ID and email shared by the provider are stored in Firebase (Apple lets you hide your email). A guest profile stays on your device only.
  • Profile backup (Firebase Realtime Database): When signed in, your display name, avatar, level / XP, statistics, coin and diamond balances, market inventory (felt, card back, frame), daily login streak, and mission and achievement progress are backed up with your account. Only you can read this backup.
  • Leaderboard: After an online match, your display name, avatar, and the number of matches won and played are written to the leaderboard. This information is public.
  • Online tables: For your seat, your account ID, display name, avatar, frame and connection status are stored, along with table settings and game state. Display name and avatar are visible to players at the table; the host's name of a public table appears in the table list. Your cards are not shown to other players; because the host's device runs the game, card data passes through that device.
  • Communication at the table: There is no chat; no message content is collected.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): If you allow notifications, your device's notification token is held by Firebase and used to send announcements; it is not linked to your account. Reminders on days you don't play are scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to a server; turn them off in Settings → Reminders. You can turn off notification permission in your phone settings at any time.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Interstitial ads appear between hands and at the end of a match, and optional rewarded ads give free coins or double the daily reward; there are no banners. AdMob and its mediation partners (Unity Ads, ironSource) may process device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. A consent form is shown in the European Economic Area and the UK; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad preferences. On iPhone, app tracking (ATT) permission may be requested; if you decline, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. Buying “Remove ads” turns off interstitial ads.
  • Purchases: The only purchase is “Remove ads”, sold on iPhone through the App Store; the Android version has no purchases. Payment and card details are handled by the store; the app never sees them and only stores on your device whether you own the item.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): To improve the game: app opens, matches started / finished, mode played, rewarded ads watched and taps on our other games in the lobby, plus device / app information (model, operating system, version, approximate country). Not linked to your account and not sold.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): If the app crashes, the error log, device model, operating system and app version are sent. Used only to fix bugs; your account ID is not included.
  • Stored on your device: Sound, music, vibration, language and reminder preferences and your profile are stored on the device and removed when you delete the app.

Data we do not collect

  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.
  • Personal data is not sold.

Coins, diamonds and gambling

Coins and diamonds are virtual units used only in the game; they cannot be purchased, converted into real money or prizes, or transferred between players. The app does not offer real-money betting or gambling.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Realtime Database, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging
  • Google AdMob, Unity Ads, ironSource — ad serving
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In — authentication
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution; the App Store also handles in-app purchase

Retention

  • Table records are deleted when the match ends or the table closes. Tables left unfinished by a lost connection may remain on the server for some time; you can request deletion by email.
  • Your profile backup and leaderboard entry are kept until you delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Data deletion

You can delete your account inside the game: tap your player card in the lobby (or Settings → Profile) → Account → Delete my account. Once you confirm, your account (Firebase Authentication), profile backup and leaderboard entry are deleted immediately and your username becomes available again. Username accounts must enter their password; Apple and Google accounts confirm in the provider's sheet, and the Apple token is also revoked.

If you can't access the app, email your username to unal.m1991@gmail.com; requests are fulfilled within 30 days. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page.
