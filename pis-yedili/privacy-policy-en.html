Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Pis Yedili Online (“the app”, iOS bundle com.pis7.pis7OnlineGame, Android package com.pis7.pis7_online_game) is a free card game developed by Mehmet Ünal. You can play against bots as a guest; online tables, friends and the leaderboard require an account. This page explains which data is used and why.

Data we collect

  • Account (Firebase Authentication): You can create an account with a username and password; the username is technically mapped to an address like username@pis7.app and no real email is required. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, the account ID and email shared by the provider are stored in Firebase (Apple may hide your email). A guest session stays on the device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Profile (Cloud Firestore): Username, display name, avatar, level / XP, stats (matches played / won), chip and diamond balance, shop inventory (deck, felt, frame), daily login streak, mission and achievement progress. Your display name, avatar and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard and at the table.
  • Online status (Firebase Realtime Database): While the app is open, an “online” flag and the time it last changed. It is shown only to signed-in players as a green dot in the friends list, on the leaderboard and at the table. It switches to “offline” when you close the app or send it to the background.
  • Game data: Tables, your seat, table settings, game state and move log, match history. The cards in your hand are stored so that only you can read them.
  • Friends and invites: The friends you add by username and table invitations.
  • Communication at the table: There is no free-text chat. Only preset messages (like “Well played!”) and smile / gift reactions are shown to players at that table.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): If you allow notifications, your device's push token is stored with your account. It is used only when someone adds you as a friend or invites you to a table; no marketing notifications are sent. Delivery goes through a small server running on Google Apps Script. Reminders on days you don't play are scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to a server; you can turn them off in Settings → Hatırlatmalar (Reminders). You can disable notifications anytime in your phone's settings.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Interstitial ads between hands and at the end of a match, and optional rewarded ads to earn chips or double your daily reward; there are no banners. The game pauses while an ad is shown. AdMob may process device identifiers (Android advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. On iPhone the app does not request tracking (ATT) permission and IDFA is not used for tracking. If you buy “Remove ads”, interstitial ads are no longer shown.
  • Purchases: Chip packs and “Remove ads” are sold through the App Store / Google Play. Payment and card details are handled by the store; the app never sees card details and only updates your balance based on the purchased product.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): To improve the game: app opens, screens viewed, matches started / finished, tables created, smiles sent and rewarded ads watched; together with your user ID, display name, whether you are a guest, and device / app info (model, OS, version, approximate country). Never sold.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): If the app crashes, the error log, device model, OS, app version and your account ID are sent. Used only to fix bugs.
  • Stored on the device: Sound, music, vibration and reminder preferences and the guest profile stay on the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Data we do not collect

  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.
  • No free-text chat; message content is not collected.
  • Personal data is never sold.

Chips and gambling

Chips and diamonds are virtual in-game units only. They cannot be exchanged for real money or prizes and cannot be sold between players. The app does not offer real-money betting or gambling.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging
  • Google AdMob — ad serving
  • Google Apps Script — notification delivery and cleanup of temporary data
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In — authentication
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution and in-app purchases

Retention

  • Abandoned tables and game records are deleted automatically after 2 days, table invites after 1 day.
  • Match history keeps the last 100 matches per player; older ones are deleted.
  • Push tokens unused for 270 days are deleted.
  • Profile, friends list, stats and balances are kept until you delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app: Settings (gear icon in the lobby) → Hesap (Account) → Hesabımı sil (Delete my account). Once you confirm, your account (Firebase Authentication) and your cloud data — profile, username, balances, stats, leaderboard entry, match history, friends list, invites sent to you, push token and online status — are deleted immediately, and you are removed from open tables. Username accounts re-enter their password; Apple / Google accounts confirm with the provider sheet, and the Sign in with Apple token is revoked.

If you can't access the app, email unal.m1991@gmail.com with your username; the request is completed within 30 days. Data on the device is removed when you uninstall the app.
