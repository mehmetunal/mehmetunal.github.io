Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Pis Yedili (“the app”, bundle / package: com.pis7.pis7OnlineGame / com.pis7.pis7_online_game) is a free mobile card game by Mehmet Ünal. Guest play supports local/bot matches; online features require Firebase Auth with a username.

Data we collect

  • Account (Firebase Auth): Username (mapped to username@pis7.app) and password for online tables. Guest sessions stay on device only.
  • Profile (Firestore): Display name, avatar, score / XP, cosmetics inventory (deck, felt, frame), and coin / diamond balances.
  • Game data (Firestore): Open table list, player status, public table / game state, and short-lived smile reactions. Hand cards are stored privately per player.
  • Local settings: Preferences and guest profile may be stored on device (SharedPreferences).
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Interstitial ads may show after matches. No banners or mid-game ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and measurement data. Test ad units may be used in development.

Data we do not collect

  • No location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.
  • No leaderboard chat messages.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Auth, Firestore
  • Google AdMob — ads
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Deleting data

Uninstalling removes local data. For account / cloud deletion requests, contact us by email.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is published on this page.
