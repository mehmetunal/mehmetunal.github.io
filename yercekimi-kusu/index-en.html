Yerçekimi Kuşu

Tap, flap, fly through the pipes!

A one-tap flying game for phones and tablets. Tap anywhere on the screen to flap; dodge pipes, snapping flowers, boss monsters, and gusts of wind while chasing your best score.

  • 3 characters: Yellow Bird (double jump), Chicken (glide), Eagle (shield)
  • Difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard, Endless
  • Obstacles: breakable and wavy pipes, snapping flowers, a boss every 10 levels, wind
  • Coins and power-ups: shield, slow motion, magnet
  • Worlds: Forest, Space, Underwater, Lava Cave · day/night and weather effects
  • Offline save · no account required
  • AdMob interstitial (end of game only, no banners)
