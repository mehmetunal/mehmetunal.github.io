Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Yerçekimi Kuşu (“the app”; iOS bundle: com.mehmetunal.yercekimiKusu, Android package: com.mehmetunal.yercekimi_kusu) is a free mobile arcade game developed by Mehmet Ünal. No personal account is required.

Data we collect

  • Local game data: Best score, games played, selected character, difficulty, world, theme, and settings are stored only on your device (SharedPreferences). Nothing is sent to our servers.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): An interstitial ad is shown at the end of a game: after the first game, then once every 4 games. No banner ads. The number of finished games is kept on the device to control ad frequency. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad delivery and measurement.

Data we do not collect

  • No account creation, email registration, or location tracking.
  • No access to camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.
  • No in-app purchases.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data deletion

Uninstall the app or clear app data in device settings to remove local game data.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is published on this page.
