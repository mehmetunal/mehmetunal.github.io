Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

KartGO Online Cards (“the app”, device name: KartGo, bundle: com.unogame.unoGame) is a free mobile card game by Mehmet Ünal. Online features use Firebase; you can sign in with username/password, Google, or Apple.

Data we collect

  • Local settings: Player name, avatar, sound/pace preferences may be stored on device (SharedPreferences).
  • Account (Firebase Auth): A user ID for creating/joining online rooms. Google or Apple sign-in may use basic profile info from the provider.
  • Game data (Firestore): Room info, player list, public table state, chat messages, and (visible only to the owner) hand cards. Related data is deleted when a room closes.
  • Stats: Optional leaderboard summaries such as matches played (tied to the player ID).
  • Analytics (Firebase Analytics): Events such as screen views, room create/join, match start/end.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Optional interstitial ads after matches and rewarded ads. Ad interaction and device identifiers may be used for ad delivery; no contact information is collected.

Data we do not collect

  • No location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.
  • Email is not required (Google / Apple sign-in is optional).

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Auth, Firestore, Analytics
  • Google AdMob — ad delivery
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution

Trademark note

This is an independent project inspired by classic matching-card games. UNO is a trademark of Mattel. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mattel.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Deleting data

Local data can be removed by uninstalling the app. Online rooms and chat are cleared when a room closes. For account/stats deletion requests, contact us by email.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is published on this page.
